Primary: spawn or resume exactly one native worker with the approved contract or delta; remain read-only.

Worker: implement the supplied contract as the sole repository writer.

Never plan, reinterpret, expand, reduce, or change requirements.

Reconcile the owner and coupled path with the current contract. Remove superseded representations and tests for removed behavior; revise proof for retained behavior.

The worker owns implementation, behavioral tests, and verification.

After a stable candidate:

1. The worker launches fresh independent assurance agents in parallel with one non-overlapping focus each; include browser acceptance for affected rendered behavior.
2. The worker receives and corrects the complete deduplicated batch at its earliest shared causes.
3. The worker launches a fresh cleanup audit and corrects its complete batch.
4. The worker launches a fresh final audit, corrects material findings, and reruns only affected focuses with fresh agents.
