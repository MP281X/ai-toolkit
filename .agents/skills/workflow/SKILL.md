## Workflow

Own reusable workflow improvement. Give each behavior one responsible owner: universal conduct and communication in `AGENTS.md`, runtime routing in Primary, role-specific depth in that role, capabilities in configuration, and mechanical policy in static enforcement. Keep metadata trigger-only.

Delete conflicting, duplicated, explanatory, default, superseded, and inactive instructions. Do not retain tutorials, planned cleanup, overlapping ownership, or verifier-owned implementation work.
